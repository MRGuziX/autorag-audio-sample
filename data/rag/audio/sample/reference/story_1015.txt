Little Timmy loved to help his big brother Trevor deliver flowers around their neighborhood. One sunny afternoon, they were counting the beautiful carnations in each bouquet, the four delivering them to their lucky recipients. Trevor, asked Timmy, how many carnations do we have in all these bouquets? Trevor replied, well, little bro, let's add them up. There are nine in the first one, 14 in the second, and 13 in the third. They started adding the numbers together on a piece of paper. After a minute or two, Timmy said excitedly, That makes 36! Curious Timmy wanted to know more. But Trevor, how many carnations will be in each bouquet on average, he wondered. With a smile, Trevor explained, we can figure out the average by dividing the total number of carnations by the number of bouquets. So, 36 divided by 3 equals 12, as they continued their delivery route. Handing out smiles along with the colorful bouquets, something unexpected happened. Suddenly, some mischievous squirrels appeared from nowhere and stole a few carnations right out of the last bouquet. That left only 10 carnations instead of 12. Timmy looked disappointed and asked, oh no, what happened to our perfect average? Trevor gently responded, sometimes things don't go exactly according to plan, even when we use math to predict them. Arr, arr, arr, arr, arr, arr, arr. Life has its surprises, both good and bad. But remember, learning from those moments helps us grow smarter and stronger every day. So although the final bouquet didn't meet their calculated expectations, Timmy still learned an important lesson about averages and adapting to unforeseen circumstances. A great reminder for any blossoming scientist.
